💓 Blood Pressure (BP)

BP measures force of blood flowing in arteries.

⚠️ Low BP

  • Dizziness
  • Weakness

🚨 High BP

  • Headache
  • Chest Pain

🧠 BP Medical Knowledge

1. Systolic Pressure

Pressure when heart contracts and pumps blood.

2. Diastolic Pressure

Pressure when heart relaxes between beats.

3. Hypertension (High BP Physiology)

  • Narrow or stiff blood vessels
  • Extra salt and fluid in body
  • Stress hormones (adrenaline)
  • Kidney problems
  • Obesity / inactivity

4. Hypotension (Low BP Causes)

  • Dehydration
  • Blood loss
  • Weak heart pumping
  • Medicine side effects
  • Sudden standing up

5. Diagnostics (How BP is Checked)

  • Sphygmomanometer (BP machine)
  • Multiple readings
  • Home monitoring
  • 24-hour ambulatory monitoring
  • Blood & ECG tests

6. Normal BP Range

  • Normal: < 120/80
  • Elevated: 120–129 / <80
  • Stage 1: 130–139 / 80–89
  • Stage 2: 140+/90+
  • Low BP: <90/60
